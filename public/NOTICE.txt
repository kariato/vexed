This source-informed TypeScript implementation modifies/reexpresses Palm Vexed
gameplay routines. Original game copyright:
  James McCombe (1999)
  The Vexed Source Forge Project Team (2006)
Original source location, exact archive hashes and modifications are documented
in docs/compatibility and docs/development/PHASE-1.md.
The port separates deterministic state from Palm drawing, sound and event APIs.
Original notices and GPLv2 text are retained under LICENSES and LICENSE.

The game uses newly authored CSS artwork and newly derived Classic solution paths.
All 48 archived packs are included at the project owner’s explicit direction.
Other solution paths are converted from archived PDBs. Their separate adaptation
terms remain unresolved; see public/PACK_NOTICES.txt (PACK_NOTICES.txt on the site).
The application GPL license does not relicense historical pack data.
Original themed titles remain outside the runtime.
The runtime Classic pack contains 59 GPL-traced layouts, generic labels and par counts.
See docs/development/PHASE-2.md for conversion/provenance and compatibility changes.
The supplied planning documents keep their original status; this notice does not
retroactively assign licensing terms to their text.

Runtime dependency notices are retained in public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

The published build includes vexed-source.tar.gz with corresponding application
source, licenses, pack notices and BUILD.txt instructions. Raw research fixtures
and original Palm binaries are excluded; converted historical paths are included.
